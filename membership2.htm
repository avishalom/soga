Why Join SOGA?

Experience the thrill of soaring to cloud base, focusing solely on finding the next thermal. Beyond flying, SOGA offers:

  • Glider Storage
  • Camping Facilities
  • Large Glider Set-up Area
  • Glider Rentals
  • Camaraderie
  • Cross-country Flight Mentorship
  • Social Events
  • Free Seminars
  • Access to Members-only Facebook Page

Membership Details

Join Now! Visit our Contact page for more information and our email address.

Waiver: All active pilots must download, sign, and submit a current waiver before their first flight of the year. Email a photo of both pages of the signed 2023 waiver to Kevin Thomson at SOGA@bell.net. No membership will be considered active without a signed waiver and active HPAC membership. Download the 2023 Waiver here.

Fee Structure for 2023:

  • Club Membership Fees: $400 if paid on or before May long weekend; $500 if paid after May 24.
  • Tow Fees: $40 per standard 2000' tow; $25 for partial tows below 1,500'; additional $12.50 per 1,000' above 2,000', provided no pilots are waiting to launch.
  • Glider Storage: Membership includes storage for one glider; additional glider storage is $75.

We look forward to seeing you at the field this flying season.

Types of Membership: Full and Associate memberships are available. Non-members can subscribe to the SOGA Facebook page to gain insight into our club.

Who Can Join: Anyone can join as an associate member. To join SOGA as a full aerotow member, you must be at least a Novice rated pilot with an aerotow sign-off and have current HPAC insurance.

How To Join: Connect with other members to explore possibilities, attend a monthly meeting, visit our field, and watch our operations. If interested, do not hesitate to contact us.

Training Requirements: Joining SOGA may require training, such as an Aerotow course. SOGA offers fee reductions to new members who complete this training to join. Contact us for more details.

Board of Directors: There are four positions on SOGA's Board of Directors: President, Vice-President, Secretary, and Treasurer.

Standing Committees: Public Relations and Safety.

Monthly Meetings: The membership meets once a month, usually on the 2nd Tuesday of every month. Guests are welcome to attend.

Rates: Annual membership is for the calendar year. See above for details.
