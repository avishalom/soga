The Skydog Report

Saturday July 28, 2016 We had a beautiful day on the Thames with Holly and Craig Raffle and their two sons Jake and Eli. We paddled from Springbank dam to Komoka bridge and the water level was the lowest that I have seen so we were stuck on the rocks a few times. Jake and Eli glided over the shallow spots with ease. With the slower flow we took five hours to make it to Komoka so we were all happy to see that bridge.
