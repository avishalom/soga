Kayak Trips 2017

The Skydog Report

Tuesday May 16, 2017 We had rain in the morning and by noon the sun was shinning so Maureen and I took Sheri for a trip from Springbank Dam to Kilworth bridge. The water level was about 18 inches higher that summer levels making the rapids a lot of fun. Sheri was looking for a flat water trip but she enjoyed our trip with  some calm water and some good rapids.
