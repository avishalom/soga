2012

Sunday November 18, 2012 Amy and Darren took a trip on the Thames from Springbank dam to Killworth Bridge and had a great sunny 52 degree Fahrenheit day with water levels about a foot below summer levels but the water was moving quickly so we brushed by some big rocks. The highlight of our trip was seeing a bald eagle that hikers pointed out to us. We finished just after dark at 5:30 pm.
