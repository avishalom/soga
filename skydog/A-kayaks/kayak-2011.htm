June 22

Wednesday June 22, 2011 we had a great group of six and the weather shaped up nicely after a cloudy day and we had sunshine for most of the trip. Mike Kovach guided our trip from Springbank Dam to Killworth and through the rapids at Killworth bridge. Jenna and Gabbie were new to kayaking and enjoyed the trip and learned a lot about short boats and how to use them. Wayne Huisman, Maureen Grant and myself had a great time.

May 23

Monday, May 23, 2011 we took a trip on the Thames from the dam in Springbank Park to Killworth Bridge with Melanie and Chantal and a great trip it was. Partly sunny and the water level was about three feet higher than summer levels so the water was moving at about 5 mph making some great rapids along the way. Maureen drove the retrieve vehicle and we all had a great time.
