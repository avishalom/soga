The Skydog Report

Green Swamp Challenge Photos & Stories Friday March 23, 2018

 Friday March 23, 2018 and Maureen and I go to Quest Air to take photos at The Green Swamp Sport Klassic. I set up my T2C and at one pm Jon Thompson tows me up and into a nice thermal at the north end of the field and I take it up to 3 grand and go searching for others and get down to 900 feet but finally catch another thermal and take it up to 2,800 feet. I am able to bobble around for a total of 51 minutes and the view with sixty gliders below waiting to get the day started at two o'clock was amazing. As I landed the comp pilots were taking off and Maureen and I grabbed cameras and were able to catch most of the take offs. Many of the comp pilots were able to get up and away with only a few coming in for relights. It will take me a few days to go through the many photos and post them here.

The Skydog Report

Green Swamp Challenge Photos & Stories Sunday March 18, 2018

 Sunday March 18, 2018 a foggy morning and Maureen and I are off to Quest Air to get some shots from the Green Swamp Challenge as this will be the first day of competition. We arrived at Quest at 9:30 expecting to get some face shots and that maybe by eleven o'clock we may get some take off shots but we later found that they were starting take offs ay 2 pm and I wanted to fly at Wallaby as my glider is set p there so we got our face shots and went back to Wallaby. I got ready to fly and at 2 pm Mikey Barber was ready so we started a line of eager pilots and Kate from Lookout Mt Tenn. was one of them. The competition pilots would likely be arriving at Wallaby just after three so I was ready with two Gopro cameras on my glider. I was off first with Mikey right behind and then Brian Jackson and a few others. Malcolm suggested that Kate wait until later in the day as it was pretty rowdy at 2:30. The cumulous were abundant and the lift was strong and rasty but I hung on and reached 4,747 feet ato and was getting sucked up hard trying to keep below the base. I did manage to get down to a safe level at 4,200 feet and we all zoomed around near base. I was fortunate enough to film three large birds either mating close by or maybe just being playful. After an hour or so I started to see comp pilots coming from the west along Hyw 33 so I headed west to meet them and the farther I got to the west the more kept coming. I was lucky enough to keep well above them as they were driving hard to get to Wallaby in good time. I was able to get some video of many of the Comp. pilots arriving and landing. After 1 hour and 40 minutes I was whipped and landed and was greeted my more than 20 pilots in the Wallaby Ranch Landing area. It was a great day for the first day of the comp and there were many happy faces at Wallaby Ranch this evening. Maureen captured many landings and Happy Faces Below. We also have some Arial video and it will be available later.
