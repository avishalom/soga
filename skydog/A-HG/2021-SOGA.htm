Soga Canada 2021

The Skydog Report

Because of Covid 19 we have been slow to start but finally Patrick and I ventured to the SOGA field on June 20th. We had 12 pilots flying today and I was able to sustaine flight for one hour and 10 minutes in my only flight of the day. My altitude was up to 4,300 feet ATO. Patrick took two 20 minute flights. Others did better later in the day but I had the longest flight and Milko was up to 4,600 feet. It was a fun day and great to be out flying again.

July 4th we try it again but this time only twenty minute flights for most.
