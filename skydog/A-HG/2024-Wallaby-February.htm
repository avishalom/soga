The Skydog Report

We took two days for Hang Gliding at Wallaby Ranch. February 2, and 4, 2024. The conditions were Not Stellar but we had some fun trying to stay aloft. My longest flight was 1 hour and 32 minutes minutes and up to 4,280 feet after getting down to 1,800 feet so it felt good to get up after getting low.. Oded Kalir had a similar flight on our second day. Jason Williams had the best flight of the day at one hour and thirty minutes and up to 5 grand above Wallaby Ranch on Tuesday.

February 25, 2024 we had similar conditions to the story above.

 Thanks to Janice Maureen Mitchell Grant for the great photos.
