The Skydog Report

September 15, Maureen, Finnegan and I head out to SOGA field at 8am and arrive at 10:30 and we are the first ones to set up. We get set up and ready to fly taking off at 3pm. My Flight lasted 1 Hour and 37 Minutes. I was down to 1,100 feet ATO a few times but was lucky enough to find another thermal that usually took me back up to 3 grand, We had 9 pilots out today. I think that Kenny Kinzie had just over two hours for the longest flight of the day. We had a bit of a late start due to maintenance on the Dragonfly. Another challenging day.

 

I could use some help with the names of the pilots flying their gliders.

The Skydog Report

August 13, Maureen, Finnegan and I head out to SOGA field at 8am and arrive at 10:30 to find a few gliders being set up. We get set up and ready to fly taking off at 1pm. My first Flight lasted 27 minutes and then a 1 hour and 14 minutes up to 4,690 feet ATO. I thermalled with Rod Regier for ten minutes pretty close and that was fun and finally Rod skied out above me. Rod had the longest flight of 3 hours and most hung out for two hours. A good challenging day.
