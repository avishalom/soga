The Skydog Report

We took two days for Hang Gliding at Wallaby Ranch. Monday and Tuesday February 13, and 14, 2023. The conditions were Not Stellar but we had some fun trying to stay aloft. My longest flight was 42 minutes and up to 2,700 feet after getting down to 1,200 feet so it felt good to get one good thermal. Oded Kalir had a similar flight on our second day. Jason Williams had the best flight of the days at one hour and thirty minutes and up to 3 grand above Wallaby Ranch on Tuesday. Thanks to Janice Maureen Mitchell Grant for the great photos.
