SOGA CANADA

2020

The Skydog Report

 Friday September 19, 2020 Patrick O'Donnell and I head up to SOGA field and it turns out to be the Best day of the year. Rod Reiger logged near four hours and most of us got up to 5,700 feet above the field or 7,300 feet MSL. Cloud base was often 5,500 feet ato. I flew into sink and landed with 46 minutes airtime. Should have gone again as the gang was getting high until 6pm.

Tuesday September 22nd we went again and this time only got two sledders of 19 minutes. Kenny stayed up one hour on the Atos.
