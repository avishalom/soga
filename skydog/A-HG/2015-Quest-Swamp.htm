April at Quest Air 2015

The Skydog Report

We visited Quest Air flight park and took a few photos on the first day of the 2015 Green Swamp Sport Klassic. The day was quite windy and the meet had to be stopped for safety reasons but there were a fair number of launches and I have a bunch of photos to display here. I also have a video of interviews at the bottom of this page. The photos have numbers so if you know the names please
