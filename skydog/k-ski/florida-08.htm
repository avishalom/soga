Kite Boarding Photos

2008

The first photos on this page were taken near Redington Shores close to St. Petersburg, Florida when we spent a wonderful day with Dave Peters who took us out sailing on his Sailboat and later we took Dave's outboard boat to an island where Dave showed us some gusty Kite Boarding. In the evening we watched a beautiful sunset over the Gulf of Mexico.

The second group of photos were taken on the east coast near Sebastian inlet where we rented a boat for the day and we saw many forms of wildlife and then we came across a group of Kite Boarders and Windsurfers.
