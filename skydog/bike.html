The Skydog Report

Maureen and I have been riding two or three times a week this summer and many of our rides start at Eight mile road and Adelaide Street just North of London. we usuall do a route that takes us 24 miles and sometimes we go to the west and sometimes to the east and usually north to Thirteen mile road. We had Patrick O'Donnell with us on one ride this summer and it was a day that rained pretty hard but most of our rides were dry.
