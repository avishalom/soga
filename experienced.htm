Experienced Pilots

All H2-rated pilots, and above, can join SOGA.

If you're not aerotow rated, we can help you with that. If you are, .... what are you waiting for?

Check out our Membership page for fee details and requirements.

SOGA, in conjunction with Instinct Windsports, offers an aerotow training course.

Membership Credit: Money spent on an aerotow course or a refresher at an accredited school can be credited towards your first year of membership at SOGA, up to $250.

As a qualified hang glider pilot, you can Contact Us and sign up.

For more information take some time to peruse this website, check our Contact Us page, and visit our public Facebook page.

Southwestern Ontario Gliding Association is a not-for-profit corporation registered in the province of Ontario, Canada
