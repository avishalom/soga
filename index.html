Welcome to Ontario's Mountain!

Located 20 minutes north of Orangeville, our site is in prime thermal territory. Aerotowing takes you up to 2000 feet. After releasing, you can soar higher and further. This is the ideal location geographically and considering airspace to fly cross country in any direction. Typical hang gliding flights last 2 hours. However, there's potential for 5 hours and distances up to 250km. We use the latest aerotowing techniques and equipment. This ensures the safest, most enjoyable hang gliding experience possible. We operate on weekends and some weekdays.

Come FLY with us!
