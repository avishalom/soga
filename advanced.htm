Advanced Pilots

Advanced pilots can enjoy a competitive approach to flying with like-minded pilots. Our site is a great place to discuss the finer points of cross-country flying and refine your skills.

With prime thermal territory and clear airspace, SOGA offers the perfect environment for long-distance flights and high-performance soaring.
