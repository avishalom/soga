Membership & Joining Requirements

Pilot Requirements

  • Valid HPAC Membership: Mandatory for all pilots.
    • Visitors to Canada: May opt for a 60-day temporary membership ($40 CAD).
  • Aerotow (AT) Rating: Must be signed off in your logbook.
    • No AT Rating? Take the course with Instinct Windsports ($300). If you join SOGA after completion, your first annual membership will be credited $250.
  • Currency: Have you aerotowed in the last 12 months?
    • Otherwise: A refresher course, tow, or tandem is required.
    • Exemption: Waived if you have logged >150 tows in the last 10 years.
  • SOGA Membership: Required for all pilots.
    • Distance Option: Pilots living more than a 4-hour drive away may opt for a weekend or daily membership.

Membership Types

  • Full Membership: For active aerotow-capable pilots.
  • Associate Membership: $50 CAD/year. For non-flying members. Includes camping and Facebook group access.
  • Daily/Weekend Membership: $50 CAD/day. Available for pilots living >4 hours away.

🚗 CARPOOL SPECIAL

Out-of-Province Pilots: Drive up with a friend and your "Day Fee" ($50) becomes a "Weekend Fee"—get 2-3 days for the price of one! (All Y'all)

Waiver

All active pilots must download, sign and submit a current waiver before their first flight of the year. A photo of both pages of the 2025 waiver, initialed and signed, then emailed to Kevin Thomson. SOGA@bell.net. No membership will be considered active without a signed waiver and active HPAC membership.

You can download a Waiver for 2025 here for your convenience.

Fee Structure for 2026

Club Membership Fees

  • Early Bird Rate: $450.00 (paid on or before May long weekend)
  • Regular Rate: $550.00 (paid after May 18th)

Tow Fees

  • Standard 2000' Tow: $40.00
  • Partial Tow (below 1,500'): $25.00
  • Extended Tow (above 2,000'): Additional $12.50 per 1,000' (when no pilots are waiting)

Glider Storage

  • First Glider: Included with membership
  • Second Glider: Additional $75.00

Club Organization

Board of Directors: There are 4 positions on SOGA's BoD. President, Vice-President, Secretary, and Treasurer.

Standing Committees: Public Relations and Safety.

Monthly Meetings: The membership meets once a month, usually on the 2nd Tuesday of every month. Guests are welcome to attend.

How To Join SOGA

Ready to join? Here's the process:

  1. Talk to other members to explore the possibilities to see if SOGA is right for you
  2. Come out to a monthly meeting and meet the members
  3. Visit our field and watch how we do things
  4. Download, complete and sign the SOGA Waiver Form
  5. Provide proof of current HPAC membership with insurance
  6. Pay the annual membership fee
