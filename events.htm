Coming from afar?

If you're out of province — or more than a 4-hour drive away — we have weekend memberships:

  • Pay $50 CAD as an Associate Member to join the Facebook group.
  • That very same $50 is banked and credited toward your first weekend fee — covering the entire weekend, up to 4 days.

Full members do not pay weekend fees.
